## CIA Triad

### Confidentiality

Confidentiality means preventing unauthorized people from accessing information.

### Integrity

Integrity means ensuring that information has not been changed or modified without authorization.

### Availability

Availability means making sure systems and information are accessible when needed.

## Authentication

Authentication verifies who a user is.

Example: Password or MFA.

## Authorization

Authorization determines what an authenticated user is allowed to access or do.
