\## Questions I Missed



\### Question 5 — IDS vs. IPS

Correct answer: IDS



An IDS detects suspicious activity and generates alerts. It does not normally block the activity automatically.



\### Question 8 — Separation of Duties

Correct answer: Separation of duties



Separation of duties divides sensitive responsibilities between different people so that one person does not have complete control over a sensitive process.
